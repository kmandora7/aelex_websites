ABOUT AELEX

ENGINEERED FOR
EVERYDAY PERFORMANCE.

AELEX manufactures premium architectural hardware solutions engineered to unite minimalist structural form with heavy-duty everyday endurance.

Our portfolio includes solid extruded brass hinges, precision sliding tower bolts, and high-grade stainless steel and aluminium fittings. Every component is crafted for smooth, silent friction-free operation and designed to withstand the rigorous demands of modern residential and commercial environments.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

FORM. FUNCTION.
FUTURE.

At AELEX, we believe true architectural luxury lies in the flawless integration of mechanical precision and timeless visual restraint. Every component is conceived as an integral architectural element — engineered to operate silently and endure indefinitely.

By combining high-grade solid metals with rigorous quality control, AELEX hardware transforms everyday spatial touchpoints into tactile expressions of strength, safety, and modern sophistication.
