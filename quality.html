THE AELEX STANDARD

BUILT WITH PRECISION

At AELEX, quality is not a stage in production — it is the foundation of our engineering ethos. Every architectural hinge, sliding tower bolt, and metal fitting is manufactured from premium extruded virgin brass, Grade 316 stainless steel, and high-tensile aluminium to withstand rigorous architectural demands.
